# Customer Context: Beauty Hair Coloration Category

Customer: fictional German drugstore retailer, ZenBeauty Retail.
Category: Beauty > Hair Coloration.
Goal: identify SKUs or subcategories needing action for the next category review.

Business actions in scope:
- delist or mark down low-revenue / low-market-signal products
- promote products with strong market demand but weak channel execution
- price review where channel price or margin suggests opportunity / risk
- fix availability where active products are out of stock despite positive market signals
- assortment gap or supplier follow-up for rising competitor trends

Constraints / notes:
- The dataset is synthetic but uses realistic public brand names and plausible product-line naming from memory.
- Status values use German-style labels: `aktiv` and `inaktiv`.
- Revenue is represented in two dimensions: `channel_*` for the retailer's own channel and `market_*` for wider market/category demand.
